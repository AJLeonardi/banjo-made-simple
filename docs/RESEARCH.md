# Research: how the Simply apps work, and what Banjo Made Simple takes from them

Sources are third-party reviews and Simply's own help pages (Nov 2026). Simply's apps are
native iOS/Android; nothing here copies their code, art, names or content — only the
general learning mechanics, which are common to the genre (Yousician, Fender Play, Duolingo).

## Mechanics observed

| Mechanic | Simply Piano / Guitar / Draw | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Course path** | A linear path of courses (Guitar: "Basics" → "Essentials", then split into *chords* and *lead* tracks). Each course is a series of short lessons. | Draw lets you *choose a path* (Animals / Nature / Characters) at onboarding. |
| **Lesson = short steps** | A lesson is a handful of short exercises: a brief explanation or video, then drilling one skill, then a "full song" that uses it. | Courses end in performing a song to cement the skill. |
| **Listens through the mic** | Judges each note as you play; correct notes turn a colour, a hint appears if you wait too long. | Reviewers: notes are detected well, chords less reliably. |
| **Wait mode / practice mode** | The music waits until you play the right note before moving on. Separate reduced-tempo options (85%, 95%). | Some reviewers find wait-until-correct repetitive; others love it for learning. |
| **Stars** | Each song/section is rated 1–3 stars on notes right and rhythm. Too many misses and you repeat the bars. | |
| **Songs split into sections** | Songs are broken into parts (intro, verse, chorus…) learned separately, then put together. | |
| **Song unlocks** | Songs come from the course; you unlock songs as you progress. Library songs are the same arrangements as in the lessons. | Criticised: no choice of difficulty per song. |
| **Backing tracks** | Lessons play along with a band/backing track. | |
| **Streaks / goals** | Weekly streak, encouraging messages, checkmarks, points. 5-minute "workouts" built from the level you're at. | |
| **Paywall** | Subscription after first lessons — a frequent complaint. | Out of scope: we unlock by progress only. |

## What we adopt, adapted for banjo

1. **Path of courses** from zero → rolls → left hand → backup (vamping) → tunes, and a short
   onboarding that asks your experience and places you on the path (like Draw's path choice).
2. **Lessons of 3–7 steps**: explain (with an animated fretboard instead of video) → drill one
   thing in *wait mode* → play it in time → play a tune with backing.
3. **Two detection modes** because banjo has two very different things to judge:
   * **Single-note picking** (rolls, melody, pinches) — note-by-note, like Simply Piano.
   * **Multi-string vamping** (backup chops on 2 & 4) — chord recognition (the flash-cards matcher).
4. **The fretboard always shows where to put your fingers** (left-hand finger numbers on the
   frets, right-hand T/I/M on the strings), and after each note shows **what we heard you
   play** on the same neck when it was wrong.
5. **Wait mode + tempo mode** with 60/80/100 % tempo. Stars: wait mode max ★; slowed tempo max ★★; full tempo ★★★.
6. **Sections**: every tune is split into A part / B part, learnable separately.
7. **Fix the criticised bit**: every tune has three arrangements — *Melody* (single notes),
   *Scruggs* (melody inside rolls) and *Backup* (vamping) — so the library has a difficulty choice.
8. **Trading** (bluegrass jam etiquette, our own addition): after the vamping lessons, tune
   lessons alternate — you take the break while the app plays backup, then the app takes the
   break and you vamp behind it.
9. **Awards, stars, daily streak and a daily-goal ring**, plus a *Daily Workout* that pulls 3
   quick drills from what you've already learned.
10. **Songs are earned**: each tune unlocks from a lesson (fretted tunes need the left-hand
    course) or from a star total.
